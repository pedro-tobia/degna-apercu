Description

A fitted black knit mini dress with a white pointed collar, a pearl-button placket and a knife-pleated skirt. The rib is 87% rayon and 13% polyester, with no elastane: the give comes from the rib itself. Close at the bust and hip, the skirt opens slightly below the white waistband. Short length: it falls at mid-thigh.

Composition & care
  • 87% rayon, 13% polyester
  • Imported
  • Dry clean, or hand wash cold with a mild detergent
