Description

A fitted black knit mini dress with a white pointed collar, a faux-pearl-button placket and a knife-pleated skirt. The rib is 87% rayon and 13% polyester, with no elastane: the give comes from the rib itself. Close at the bust and hip, the skirt opens slightly below the white waistband.
