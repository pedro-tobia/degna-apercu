DEGNA

Pedro Tobia is an artistic director for several clothing houses, among them the RADICE collection of Maison Godefroi, shown at the Héritage runway show in Montréal in September 2026. DEGNA is the house he founded to set out his own vision of women.

A vision made of timeless collections: pieces that follow the woman who wears them, not the season. The DEGNA silhouette starts from one idea of elegance, and of the heritage every woman leaves behind her, wherever she goes. Degna, in Italian, means worthy.

Style is the reflection of your personality: our silhouette says who we are before we speak. I like the idea of leaving an elegant trace, a heritage, wherever I go and in every encounter.

Pedro Tobia, founder and artistic director

On every DEGNA piece there is a pearl. It is the touch of elegance: the last gesture, the one you notice without it ever insisting. A pearl is born slowly, layer by layer, around a single grain: it is the image of the heritage a woman leaves behind her. The pearl will stay, from one collection to the next: it is the thread of the house.

Sempre is cut in one rib knit, with contrast trims and pearl buttons, in pieces designed to be worn together.
