Shipping & exchanges

Shipping

  • Free standard shipping to the US and Canada, 10–15 business days.
  • Express to the US and Canada, 5–10 business days: $29.
  • Every order ships from the United States. Duties included: nothing to pay on delivery, in the US or in Canada.

Exchanges

  • We do not offer refunds: we exchange.
  • Free size exchanges within 30 days of delivery. A prepaid label is included in the box.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags attached.
  • To start an exchange, write to hello@degna.ca with your order number and the size you need.
  • Damaged or incorrect item: write to hello@degna.ca. We replace it, or refund it if we cannot.

Duties & taxes

  • Every order ships from the United States.
  • Duties are included for the US and Canada: nothing to pay on delivery.
  • Sales tax is calculated at checkout where applicable.
  • Prices are in US dollars.
