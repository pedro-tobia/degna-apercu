Shipping & exchanges

Shipping

  • Made for you: every piece ships within 2 weeks of your order, from Los Angeles. Cancel at any time before it ships for a full refund.
  • Free standard shipping once shipped: 3–7 business days in the US, 5–10 to Canada.
  • Express: $29, 2–3 business days in the US.

Returns & exchanges

  • Free returns within 30 days of delivery: you are refunded within 14 days of our receiving the item, to your original payment method.
  • Free size exchanges within 30 days of delivery. A prepaid label is included in the box.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags attached.
  • To start an exchange, write to hello@degna.ca with your order number and the size you need.
  • Damaged or incorrect item: write to hello@degna.ca. We replace it, or refund it if we cannot.

Duties & taxes

  • Orders ship from Los Angeles.
  • United States: no customs duties, your order ships within the country.
  • Canada: DEGNA pays the import duties and taxes. Nothing to pay on delivery.
  • Sales tax is calculated at checkout where applicable.
  • Prices are in US dollars.
