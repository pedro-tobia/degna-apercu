Privacy

  • Person responsible for the protection of personal information: Pedro Tobia, founder, hello@degna.ca.

  • Order and payment details are held by Stripe, our payment provider, for as long as the law requires.
  • You may ask to access, correct or delete your information at any time by writing to the address above.
