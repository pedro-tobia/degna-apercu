Shipping & returns

Shipping

  • Free standard shipping to the US and Canada, 2–4 business days.
  • Express to the US, 1 business day: $25.
  • US orders ship from inside the US: no duties to pay on delivery.

Returns

  • Free returns within 30 days of delivery.
  • A prepaid return label is included in the box.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags attached.
  • Refunds are issued within 3 business days of receipt.
  • To exchange, return the item and place a new order.
  • Damaged or incorrect item: write to hello@degna.ca.

Duties & taxes

  • US orders ship from inside the US: no duties on delivery.
  • Canadian orders ship from Montréal.
  • Sales tax is calculated at checkout where applicable.
  • Prices are in US dollars.
